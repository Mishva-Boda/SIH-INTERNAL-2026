## 🌟 Key Features

### 🧠 1. AI Multi-Vector Psychometric Assessment
- Evaluates career fit across **12 aptitude vectors** including Mathematics, Logical Reasoning, Spatial Awareness, and Verbal Ability.
- Mapped dynamically to real Indian job market demand and higher education options.

### 📚 2. Class 11 Stream Selector Engine
- Helps Class 10 students choose between **Science (PCM/PCB), Commerce, and Arts/Humanities**.
- Aligned with **CBSE, ICSE, and State Board** curricula and exam timelines (JEE, NEET, CUET, CLAT, NATA, NDA).

### 🏛️ 3. Smart College Finder & Cutoff Rank Predictor
- Browse top **NIRF-ranked institutes** across 28 Indian States & UTs.
- Categorized by **📍 Near You in your city**, **🏛️ Recommended in your State**, and **⭐ Best Matches Across India**.
- Simulate admission probabilities for JEE Main, NEET, CUET, and state CET exams based on Category and All India Rank (AIR).

### 💰 4. Smart Scholarship & Grant Matcher
- Live tracking for **300+ national, state, corporate, and merit-cum-means educational grants** (Reliance Foundation, Tata Trust, NSP, INSPIRE DST).
- Real-time eligibility checking by income bracket, category, and state quota.

### 🗺️ 5. Interactive Skill Gap & Milestone Roadmaps
- Visual zig-zag learning timelines with step-by-step topic checklists.
- Real-time percentage progress tracking and milestone completion counts (`X of Y Topics Completed`).

### 👥 6. Multi-Role Dashboards
- **Student Dashboard**: Personalized dashboard with active career matches, roadmap milestones, and scholarship links.
- **Parent Portal**: Progress tracking for parents via secure linked access codes.
- **Teacher Dashboard**: Class-wide analytics, stream distribution charts, and student assessment monitoring.
